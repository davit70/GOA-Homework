The Greatest People In History

Alexander the Great

Alexander the Great (Alexander III of Macedon) was one of history's most successful military commanders. He created one of the largest empires in the ancient world by the age of 30. Born in Pella in 356 BC, he was tutored by Aristotle and became king in 336 BC. During his 13-year reign, he never lost a single battle.

Achievements

  • Never lost a battle.
  • Created one of the largest empires.
  • Spread Greek culture across Asia.
  • Founded over 20 cities.

Julius Caesar

Julius Caesar was a Roman general and statesman who played a critical role in the rise of the Roman Empire. His military victories and political reforms changed Roman history forever.

Achievements

  1. Conquered Gaul.
  2. Expanded Roman territory.
  3. Introduced important reforms.
  4. Influenced the future of Rome.

Fun Facts

History is filled with great leaders who changed the world through leadership, military skill, science, and discovery.
