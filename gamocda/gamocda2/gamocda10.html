koenigsegg jesko


The Koenigsegg Jesko is a limited-production, track-focused megacar powered by a 5.0-liter twin-turbocharged V8 that produces 1,280 hp on standard gasoline and 1,600 hp on E85 biofuel. Paired with a 7-clutch, 9-speed Light Speed Transmission (LST), it is available in two variants: the high-downforce Attack and the ultra-low drag Absolut
