BMW M4 was not invented by a single individual, but rather developed by BMW M GmbH





  • Horse Power - Base M4: 473 hp and 406 lb-ft of torque, mated to a 6-speed manual transmission
  • Acceleration & Speed - Base (RWD/Manual): 0-60 mph in 4.1 to 4.4 seconds.
  • Handling & Braking - Grip: Pulls over 1.07 g on the skidpad.Braking: Stops from 60 mph in roughly 105 to 107 feet.
