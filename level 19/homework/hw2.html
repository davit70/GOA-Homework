Koenigsegg jesko


The Koenigsegg Jesko is a limited production mid-engine sports car produced by the Swedish automobile manufacturer Koenigsegg Automotive AB. The car was introduced at the 2019 Geneva Motor Show and was completely sold out before the show ended.[4][5][6] Succeeding the Agera, the Jesko is named as a tribute to the company founder's father, Jesko von Koenigsegg.[7] There are three variations of the car: the "Absolut", "Attack", and "Sadair's Spear".[8]
