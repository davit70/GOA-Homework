Dodge Challenger

The Dodge Challenger is an iconic, retro-styled American muscle car known for its roomy interior, straight-line performance, and deep V8 exhaust note. After a legendary run, the final gas-powered Challenger models rolled off the assembly line in December 2023, making way for the electrified Dodge Charger successor
